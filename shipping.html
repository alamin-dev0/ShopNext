Delivery Time

Delivery time may vary depending on your location, product availability, courier capacity and other circumstances.

  • Inside Dhaka: usually 1–3 business days.
  • Outside Dhaka: usually 2–5 business days.
  • Remote areas may require additional time.
  • Orders placed during holidays may take longer.

How Your Order Is Delivered

After you place an order, the order goes through several stages before reaching you.

  • New: Your order has been received.
  • Confirmed: The order has been checked and confirmed.
  • Processing: Products are being prepared.
  • Packed: Your order has been packed.
  • Shipped: The courier has received the shipment.
  • Delivered: The order has reached you.

Delivery Address

Please provide a complete and accurate delivery address during checkout.

  • Full name
  • Active phone number
  • City / area
  • House / road / building information
  • Additional delivery instructions if required

An incorrect or incomplete address may cause delivery delays.

Failed Delivery

If the delivery cannot be completed because the customer is unavailable, the phone number is unreachable or the address is incorrect, the courier may attempt delivery again according to their policy.

Please keep your phone available around the expected delivery time.

Important Information

  • Delivery time is an estimate and is not always guaranteed.
  • Product availability may affect dispatch time.
  • Some products may have different delivery conditions.
  • Courier delays may occur because of weather, holidays or unexpected situations.
  • Always check your order details for the latest available status.
