Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information ShopNext may collect, how we use it, and how we work to protect your information when you use our website and services.

🛡️ Last updated: September 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use ShopNext, we may collect information that is necessary to provide our shopping and customer support services.

Account Information

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Account login information

Order Information

  • Products you order
  • Delivery address
  • Billing information
  • Order notes
  • Order and delivery details

Communication Information

If you contact us through our contact form, email, phone, SMS or another supported communication method, we may keep the information necessary to respond to your request.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information only for legitimate business and service purposes, including:

  • Creating and managing customer accounts
  • Processing orders
  • Delivering products
  • Providing order updates
  • Responding to customer support requests
  • Managing returns and refunds
  • Improving our website and services
  • Preventing fraud and unauthorized activity
We do not need your information for unrelated purposes. Our goal is to collect and use only information that is reasonably necessary to operate the store.

3. Orders & Payments

When you place an order, information such as your name, phone number, address, ordered products and payment method may be required to complete the order.

Payment Information

Depending on the payment method selected, payment processing may be handled by a third-party payment provider.

ShopNext should not directly store sensitive payment credentials such as card PINs, CVVs or online banking passwords. Such information should be handled by the appropriate payment provider.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

Our website may use cookies, browser storage or similar technologies to provide useful shopping features.

These technologies may be used for things such as remembering cart items, wishlist items, login state, preferences and other website functionality.

Important: During the current demo/frontend phase, some shopping data may be stored in the browser using LocalStorage. In the final production version, sensitive account information should be handled securely by the server and database.

5. Information Sharing

We may share necessary information with trusted service providers when required to operate the store.

Examples may include:

  • Payment providers
  • Courier and delivery companies
  • Email service providers
  • SMS notification providers
  • Hosting and infrastructure providers

Only information reasonably necessary for the relevant service should be shared.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect customer information from unauthorized access, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

In the production version of ShopNext, security measures should include secure passwords, server-side authentication, HTTPS, secure sessions, database access controls and appropriate protection against common web attacks.

Security note: Never share your account password, OTP, card PIN or other sensitive authentication information with anyone.

7. Your Rights

Depending on applicable laws and circumstances, customers may have rights regarding their personal information.

  • Request access to certain personal information
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion where applicable
  • Ask questions about how information is used
  • Contact us regarding privacy concerns

Some information may need to be retained when required for legal, accounting, fraud-prevention or legitimate business purposes.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for users who are legally able to make purchases and enter into applicable agreements.

We do not intentionally request unnecessary personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, technology, business practices or legal requirements change.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page along with the latest update date.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, you can contact our support team.
