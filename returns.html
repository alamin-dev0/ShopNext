Returns & Refunds

We want you to be happy with your purchase. If something is not right with your order, you can request a return or refund according to our return policy.

↩️

Return Window

Return requests should normally be submitted within 7 days of receiving the product.

📦

Product Condition

Products should be unused, undamaged and preferably returned with the original packaging.

💰

Refund

Approved refunds are processed after the returned product is inspected and accepted.

Return Policy

Before requesting a return, please make sure your product meets the following conditions.

Products may be eligible for return when:
  • The product is damaged or defective.
  • The wrong product or wrong variant was delivered.
  • The product received is significantly different from the product description.
  • The return request is submitted within the applicable return period.

Products that may not be eligible

  • Products damaged because of misuse or improper handling.
  • Products that have been heavily used or altered.
  • Products returned without required accessories or components.
  • Products outside the applicable return period.
  • Certain hygiene, personal-care or customized products where return restrictions apply.

Important: Return approval is subject to product inspection. The final decision may depend on the actual condition of the product and the reason for the return.

How to Request a Return

Follow these simple steps to submit a return request.

1

Find Your Order

Open your account and go to your order history.

2

Request Return

Select the delivered order and submit your return request with the reason.

3

Product Review

Our team reviews the request and may contact you for additional information.

4

Refund

Once the return is approved, the refund process will be started according to the payment method.

Refund Policy

When will I receive my refund?

Refunds are processed after the returned product has been received and inspected. The actual time may depend on the selected payment method.

Cash on Delivery orders

For Cash on Delivery orders, the refund method will be confirmed by our support team.

Online payment orders

For online payments, approved refunds may be sent through the applicable payment provider or another agreed method.

Partial refunds

In certain situations, only part of an order may qualify for a refund. In that case, the refund will be calculated based on the eligible item or items.

Exchange Policy

Product exchange may be available for selected items, such as an incorrect size or variant, depending on stock availability.

  • Exchange requests should be submitted within the applicable return period.
  • The replacement product must be available in stock.
  • The returned item must meet the applicable condition requirements.
  • If the requested replacement is unavailable, a refund option may be offered.

Return Delivery Charges

Return delivery charges depend on the reason for the return.

  • If the product is defective or the wrong product was delivered, return delivery may be handled according to our support team's decision.
  • If the return is requested for another reason, applicable delivery or handling charges may apply.
  • Customers should contact support before sending a product back.

Order Cancellation

You may be able to cancel an order before it enters the shipping or fulfillment process.

  • New or Confirmed orders may be eligible for cancellation.
  • Processing, Packed or Shipped orders may not be cancellable through the normal cancellation option.
  • For shipped orders, please contact support for assistance.

Important Information

  • Always keep your order ID for faster support.
  • Take photos or videos of damaged products when possible.
  • Do not send a product back before receiving return instructions from our support team.
  • Refund approval is subject to verification and inspection.
  • Return and refund rules may vary depending on the product category and payment method.

Need help with a return?

Contact our support team and provide your order ID. We'll help you with the next steps.
