Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about products, orders, payments, delivery, returns and your Alamin Shop account.

🔍

Orders

Order related questions
Browse products from the Products page, open the product you want, select the quantity and click Add to Cart. Then open your cart and continue to checkout.
After placing an order, you can visit My Orders from your account. Your order ID, products, total amount, payment method and order status will be shown there.
Orders can normally be cancelled while they are still in the early stages such as New or Confirmed. Once an order has already been shipped, cancellation may no longer be available.

Payment

Payment related questions
Alamin Shop can support Cash on Delivery, bKash, Nagad, SSLCommerz and card/mobile banking payments. Actual online payment gateways will be connected during the backend/payment integration phase.
Yes. Cash on Delivery can be selected during checkout where the service is available for your delivery location.

Delivery

Shipping related questions
Delivery charges depend on the delivery zone and order value. The applicable delivery charge will be calculated and shown during checkout before you place the order.
Open your order details from My Orders. Once courier tracking is connected, the order page will also show the tracking number and shipment progress.

Returns & Refunds

Return related questions
Return eligibility depends on the product and the return policy. For eligible delivered orders, you can request a return from the order details page.
Refund timing depends on the payment method and the return/refund process. Once the refund is approved, the applicable payment provider's processing time will apply.

Account

Account related questions
Click the Account or Register option and complete your name, email, phone number and password. In the final backend version, your email will also need to be verified using an OTP.
Email verification helps confirm that the email address belongs to you. In the final system, registration will send a verification OTP to your email before your account becomes fully active.
Use the Forgot Password option on the login page. The final backend system will send a secure password-reset link or OTP to your verified email address.
